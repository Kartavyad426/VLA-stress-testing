From one failed rollout to a line in the Data Gap Manifest

The benchmark tells you the policy scored 61%. This is the part that comes after: which rollouts failed, at which phase of the manipulation, what perturbation caused it, and what data would close it.

Illustrative walkthrough — no rollouts have been run yet. Every number on this page is fabricated to show the shape of the output. Magnitudes are anchored on published LIBERO-Plus results (arXiv:2510.13626), which report success falling from ~95% to under 30% under camera-viewpoint and initial-state shifts. Real figures require the Phase 1 baseline and Phase 2 stress matrix.

STEP 1 · STRESS

Where does the operating envelope actually break?

Same policy, same tasks, same seeds. Only the perturbation dimension changes. A single aggregate success number would collapse all three of these curves into one figure.

What this buys you

Camera viewpoint crosses the failure boundary between L2 and L3; object layout never crosses it at all. That is a prioritisation decision made on evidence rather than on intuition — and it says spend the data budget on viewpoint, not on clutter.

STEP 3 · ATTRIBUTE

Counterfactual probes, not correlation

What a client actually receives

One row of the Data Gap Manifest. It has to be specific enough that a separate team can act on it without re-reading a single rollout.

DGM-001  Severity: high

LIBERO-Spatial — pick and place, 5 of 10 tasks affected
Observed failure
Visual grounding degrades under camera yaw; surfaces as repeated pre-grasp misalignment and empty-gripper closure
Trigger
Camera yaw beyond +12°, confirmed by single-dimension counterfactual probe (+58 pp on revert)
Evidence
3,000 episodes · 94.0% ± 2.1 at L0 → 34.0% ± 4.4 at L3 · boundary L2–L3
Coverage required
Demonstrations spanning yaw +10° to +20° in 2° steps, across ≥3 object layouts and both target-object classes. Include successful re-approach trajectories after a missed grasp — the current failure mode is an unrecoverable loop, not a single bad reach.
Validation plan
Fine-tune at three escalating data budgets, re-run the 312-episode frozen regression set, and report the data-response curve. A neutral or negative result is still a reportable finding.
