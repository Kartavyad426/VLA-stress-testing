2 · Loss curves

The x-axis is the optimizer step in every chart. lerobot_train logs a loss: value every 10 micro-steps, which is the mean of those 10 micro-batch losses (lerobot AverageMeter, reset at each log; third_party/lerobot/src/lerobot/scripts/lerobot_train.py:698,792). The micro-step is read from the tqdm counter (N/TOTAL) and divided by the run's accumulation, so with accumulation 8 a point lands every 1.25 optimizer steps. Cached-feature runs log the mean of the last accumulation × 10 micro-batch losses every 10 optimizer steps (experiments/r056_train.py:478). Faint dots are the raw logged values, and the solid line is the median of each block of optimizer steps. LR and gradient norm are plotted in their own panels, never on a second y-axis.
