Short answers

  • Orange (reference): the scripted expert picks, lifts, places, puts in the bowl and stacks every block, at nominal and at mass/friction ×0.5 and ×2. Its weak points are visual: in the wrist view our blocks are drawn 1–1.7 cm off the real ones (K3 reproduced), the sim table's edges sit ~3 cm inside the real ones, the wrist composite has ~1.6× the real shadow area (30% vs 19% of the table), and objects render darker than real in the exterior view.
  • The checks catch known errors: a camera moved 2 cm along its line of sight or tilted 3° makes the wrist offsets grow ~1.5–2.5 cm; a hidden object is flagged; low friction fails every expert probe; a 2 cm depth shift shows in MoGe's exterior residual. See the known-bad table.
  • The 10-27 calibration-test scenes were built from calibrations already known to be wrong (K13/K14); the checks are shown on them as a second, harder test, not as scene verdicts.
  • Not run: C6/C7's GPU parts, and every composite-based check (C1–C5, D6, contact shadows) on scenes without composite caches.
