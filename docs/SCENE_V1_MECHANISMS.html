1 · Inputs: what an episode must contain

Today
One DROID episode folder: trajectory.h5 (joints, gripper position, cartesian pose, camera extrinsics incl. the wrist camera's gripper offset), <serial>.mp4 and <serial>-stereo.mp4 per camera (two exterior ZED 2, one wrist ZED Mini), metadata_*.json (episode uuid). Camera intrinsics come from KarlP/droid's intrinsics.json keyed by the episode uuid (data/real2sim/droid/calib/, 126 MB, 72,468 episodes).
On disk
Two episodes, ep_orange_block and ep_c3 (528 MB). Both are AUTOLab, the same FR3, the same wrist camera (18026681) and the same exterior cameras (22008760, 24400334), recorded 08-21 and 09-02. c3 tests a new scene, not a new rig.
Non-DROID
lerobot_episode.py converts a LeRobot v3 episode into this layout with zero extrinsics and no stereo. Calibrating without camera information was tried on CMU (OXE) data, failed, and is parked by the user. With no stereo, §3, §4 (anchors) and §7's table check don't run.
Generalises?
To other DROID episodes: yes, the format is the same. Beyond DROID: no (stereo + known intrinsics + wrist camera are assumed throughout).

2 · Perception (frame 0) — perceive.py

Mechanism
One exterior frame (default 0). Grounding DINO tiny finds one box per text prompt (threshold 0.30 box / 0.25 text); optional --multi prompts keep several instances per label (NMS, max 15% of the image). SAM segments each box. MoGe-2 gives a depth map and normalised intrinsics. Writes perception.npz (image, depth, points, masks per label) and a check image.
Hand-set
The prompts. The default list is orange's objects (black bowl, orange block, yellow block, green block, wooden table, robot gripper). c3 was prompted by hand. vlm_objects.py (NVIDIA NIM, Llama-3.2-11B vision) lists objects in a frame and was compared against my hand prompts, but it doesn't feed perceive.
Hard-coded downstream
The label "wooden table" is required by build_scene (table fit, stereo scale), calibrate_cam (table term) and calibrate_light. A scene whose table is a white cart or a counter must still be detected under that exact label.
Generalisation risk
Medium. Detector misses and merged instances on cluttered tables; object names drive asset lookup, task wording and redaction.

3 · Stereo depth and table height — stereo_depth.py

Mechanism
DROID's stereo videos are rectified side by side. OpenCV SGBM disparity → depth with the published baselines (ZED 2 120 mm, ZED Mini 63 mm). In build_scene --depth stereo, MoGe keeps the shape and the stereo/MoGe median ratio on the table sets the scale. Stereo isn't used directly: SGBM is valid on ~40% of pixels and smears small blocks. As a check: table-top height from exterior stereo (one frame) and from wrist stereo (pose from the recorded joints, independent of the calibration) vs the built table → stereo_table_check.json.
Hand-set
Check frames 0,20,40 (must be before objects move, and the table must be visible).
Evidence
Fixed fault 1 (table 6 cm high on orange, 8 cm on c3). Orange now −0.2 cm (ext), −0.4…+0.2 cm (wrist).
Generalisation risk
Low on DROID. Texture-less or shiny tables give sparse disparity.

4 · Exterior camera calibration and wrist anchors — calibrate_cam.py, wrist_anchors.py

Outline fit
Intrinsics fixed to the ZED factory values. Real robot masks per frame (motion masks by default, or DINO "robot arm"/"robot gripper" + SAM). The 6-DoF camera pose minimises a chamfer cost between the real and rendered FR3 + 2F-85 silhouettes at the recorded joints (1/4 resolution, random search of 2000 poses, then local refinement). A sphere test first checks MuJoCo's intrinsics convention (< 1.5 px).
Wrist anchors
For chosen wrist frames, the wrist camera pose comes from FK of the recorded joints + DROID's gripper offset. Objects are detected in the wrist image (labels from the scene's perception), with stereo depth from the ZED Mini. Each object gets a median 3D point, clustered across frames within 3 cm. Detections more than 15 cm above or 3 cm below --table_z are dropped (the gripper was once labelled a block).
Anchored fit
The same optimisation plus two terms: anchor reprojection error (px) and |exterior-stereo table z − wrist-stereo table z| (cm). Orange: anchors 44.8 → 4.9 px, table 1.5 → 0 cm, outline IoU 0.488 → 0.495, 597 s.
Order (no driver)
Anchors need a built scene (for the wrist camera), and the stereo build needs a calibration. So today: perceive → outline calibration → build → replay → wrist anchors → anchored calibration → stereo build → replay → checks. This order is recorded only in logs and my notes.
Hand-set
Outline frames default to 30,45,60,72,90,110,130 (orange; frame 0 dropped because of wall flicker). Anchor frames and weights per scene. Which fit to accept was decided by hand.
Evidence of trouble
c3: anchored fit gets the table right (0.08 cm, anchors 17 px) but the outline worse (IoU 0.40 → 0.35); not accepted, unresolved. Orange's outline is only 0.49 with either fit (issue #8: arm drawn off in places; suspects intrinsics, lens distortion, robot masks; unchecked).
Generalisation risk
Highest. A wrong pose is invisible from the calibrated view and spoils everything after it (faults 1–2).

5 · Scene build — build_scene.py

Robot
Menagerie FR3 + Robotiq 2F-85, mount yaw 90° (sign unresolved, checklist 0.7), DROID wrist camera from the recorded gripper offset. Timestep 2 ms, noslip 3.
Table
Box; top = median z of the eroded "wooden table" points, extent = 2–98th percentile; floor assumed 0.75 m below (--floor_below, ASSUMED).
Objects by label
object_kind: labels with table/gripper/robot are skipped; "bowl" → bowl; "block/cube/box" → fitted box (cubes stay cubes; three or more same-label cubes are clamped to 0.5–1.5× their median size); anything else → "ellipsoid" path. Colour = median pixel of the mask.
Mesh source --assets
libero (default): bowl = LIBERO akita_black_bowl scaled to the fitted rim; other objects matched by head noun against LIBERO object names ("red radish" → radish), else a fitted ellipsoid stand-in. none: ellipsoids only. A pack (kitchen|gso|ycb or a list): name match + plausible scale (0.5–2×), the pack's own collision parts kept, the bowl only from packs that ship collision parts (to keep the cavity); no match → redacted (§6). --object_props sets mass/friction/scale per label or category; otherwise density 500 × visual hull volume.
Outputs
scene.mjb, scene.xml, scene_fit.json (every fitted number + where it came from), projection check.
Edits after build
Only by patching the compiled .mjb (refit_block.py, which must also update the body's BVH or the block sinks), plus render_colour_overrides.json for render colours. No build-time overrides file yet (decision 2).
Generalisation risk
Medium. Non-block shapes become ellipsoids or are redacted; a bowl is the only container kind.

6 · Redaction

Where it happens
build_scene.py, pack builds only. A non-box, non-bowl object with no plausible pack model, or a bowl with no open-top pack bowl, is left out of the scene. It's listed under scene_fit.json["redacted"] with its centre, width or rim radius and colour, and a WARNING is printed. Blocks/cubes/boxes are never redacted (always fitted boxes).
Who reads it
composite.redacted_discs turns each entry into a footprint disc. Used by: scene_checks (real grasps of a redacted object are skipped, n/a not FAIL); composite.wrist_plate (a cylinder over the footprint is inpainted out of the wrist frame-0 plate); composite.wrist_table_texture (texels near the footprint left out of the mosaic). Tasks: a redacted object isn't in fit["objects"], so no task names it.
Gap (a)
Exterior plate. clean_plate inpaints the masks of scene_objects() = fit["objects"] only. A redacted object is not there, so its real pixels stay in the exterior background: the policy sees an object that isn't in the physics. Fix: add the redacted labels' masks (they're in perception.npz) to the hole. Small change, my code.
Gap (b)
The wrist masking path has never run on a pack-built scene (EFFORTS §8, AssetPack leftovers). No pack-built scene has been composited or rolled out.
Gap (c)
Balls, spheres, cans, cups and cylinders are redacted when no pack matches, though MuJoCo builds them in one line. Proposed: fitted sphere/cylinder before the pack lookup (decision 1, open).
Gap (d)
The mode is not settled for v1. libero never redacts but uses research-only assets and ellipsoid stand-ins. Pack mode redacts and needs (a)–(c).
Tested
CPU only, c3 with --assets gso,ycb (AssetPack, 643c5c1): apple = YCB 013_apple, radish redacted, expert 57/60 over 12 tasks (libero: 56/65 over 13). ObjGen is working on replacing redaction with generated meshes (separate effort).
Verdict
Ready to build scenes with redaction. Not ready to render them correctly: (a) must be fixed and (b) tested before a pack-built scene goes to a policy.

7 · Replay and scene checks — replay.py, scene_checks.py

Replay
Drives the sim arm through the recorded joints (15 Hz, position actuators) and the gripper through the recorded gripper position, with contacts on. Writes replay_states.npz (the sim posed at each real frame), which almost every later step reads, and a video. Not a pass/fail criterion (user, 10-15: a digital cousin can't reproduce exact motions). --target defaults to orange_block.
Checks
2.1 settle (< 2 cm in 0.5 s at the frame-0 joints); per recorded close/open pair: 1.5 real grasp point vs nearest object (≤ 1.5 cm vertical and horizontal), 1.6 release inside the container rim, 1.7 grasp→release vs object→container distance (±10%); 1.1 stereo table (≤ 1 cm). Thresholds mine, user-accepted 10-19. Containers = free bodies with "bowl" in the name.
Hand-set
Stereo frames 0,20,40; replay target name.
Generalisation risk
Low–medium. Episodes with no grasp, or with a release onto something other than a bowl, leave 1.5–1.7 as n/a.

8 · Object size and colour check — block_size_check.py

Mechanism
Wrist frames before the first real grasp, with the sim posed at the replayed state. The real block is found by an HSV range inside a window around where the sim draws it (largest blob, sim-robot pixels excluded). Size ratio = √(real px / sim px), median over frames; real median colour next to the sim colour.
Hand-set
The HSV table is hard-coded for orange's three blocks (real "green" is teal, "yellow" is lime). Blocks only: bowls and other objects aren't measured (issue #12: bowl outline bigger and offset, unchecked).
Evidence
Found the yellow block 1.32× too small (re-fit to 3.27 cm → 1.00); green 0.88 on far frames only.
Generalisation risk
High as written. Needs masks from the detector/SAM per object in place of colour ranges.

9 · Tasks, scripted expert, do-nothing filter — make_tasks.py, scripted_grasp.ik, policy_batch --policy hold

Inventory
Bowl → "the bowl"; box → "the <colour> block" (colour named from the real median colour by hue bins; two same-colour blocks become left/right; three or more look-alikes are not targets); any other object → "the <noun>" (pack category or label noun).
Templates
put_in_bowl for every nameable movable object (needs a bowl; only the first bowl is used); stack for consecutive block pairs (≤ 3); next_to object pairs (≤ 3).
Variants
Variant 0 = the scene as built; others jitter free objects ±3 cm (bowl ±1.8 cm) and blocks ±0.5 rad yaw, with overlaps rejected. Seed = seed·1000 + task·100 + variant; it is the full rng seed (a gotcha behind the 10-20 seed bug).
Expert
Damped-least-squares IK on the Robotiq pinch site to waypoints from the sim's own poses: above object → down to its centre → close → lift → above goal → lower → open, with physics on. Top-down centre grasp for every object kind. Success from sim state (in bowl: within 0.8 rim radii and below the rim top; stacked; next to: 3–12 cm).
Filters
Only expert-solved variants enter the eval set (3.1); a --policy hold batch marks variants a do-nothing policy "solves" for --trivial_from (3.2).
Evidence
Orange 34/40 (yfit yellow tasks 9/9); c3 57/65 with the radish excluded. Stacking: expert succeeds, GR00T 0/15.
Generalisation risk
Medium–high for diverse scenes. One container kind (bowl, matched by name), one grasp (top-down centre), no tasks for drawers, lids, pouring or articulated objects; a top-down grasp on a mug or a tall bottle will often fail, so whole scenes may yield few tasks.

10 · Composite: exterior view — composite.Compositor, calibrate_light.py

Background plate
Per-pixel median over up to ~150 frames of the exterior video. The scene objects' frame-0 masks (optionally also objects re-detected in the plate) are dilated 15 px and inpainted (Telea, or LaMa). Cached per scene.
Per frame
Sim robot + object pixels (segmentation) are pasted over the plate. Objects get the real median colour of their mask (then render_colour_overrides.json).
Shadows
Render with and without robot/objects; table pixels where the ratio < 0.9 are "in shadow" and darkened to the measured real ratio (one number); a 3 px Gaussian on the ratio map. The light direction is grid-searched (azimuth × elevation) to match the real robot shadow in 7 frames (orange: IoU 0.445, darkness 0.845).
Known issues
#1: the robot is not cut from the plate, so where the arm sits in about half the frames or more its smear stays (ghost). #9: shadows sharper than real. Redacted objects not cut (§6 gap a).
Does it matter to the policy?
G3: swapping our whole exterior image for the real one did not move GR00T's plan. Low priority for GR00T; it matters for the user's review and for other policies.
Generalisation risk
Medium. Episodes where the arm stays in one place longer leave bigger ghosts; a scene with diffuse light has no robot shadow to fit.

11 · Composite: wrist view — composite.WristRenderV2

Table texture
wrist_table_texture: a top-down mosaic of the table from the real wrist video before any object is touched. Each texel is projected into each frame (sim posed at the recorded joints), weighted by cos/z², per-texel median (moving shadows drop out), one exposure pass (the ZED Mini auto-exposes). Robot pixels dilated 35 px and objects 61 px are skipped, texels near objects (+1.5 cm) are left out, and holes are inpainted with LaMa.
Fallback order
Table pixels: wrist mosaic first → wrist frame-0 plate (wrist_plate, LaMa-inpainted robot/objects) → exterior plate projected into the wrist view. The seam at the mosaic's edge is issue #6.
Gripper
gripper_library.py: per-pixel median over real wrist frames with the gripper OPEN gives a real gripper image + soft alpha, pasted over our render when the sim gripper is open (within open_tol). Closed or closing: our render with a focus blur, which since 10-23 is gripper-only (--wrist_blur gripper); the old "depth" mode blurred everything within 20 cm. Built per wrist camera (18026681); partly closed bins were rejected because held objects get baked in (issue #11).
Light and shadow
light_fit_wrist.py: real dark mask = table pixels ≥ 15% darker than our shadow-free render after exposure gain; grid over light directions; score = mean IoU with the sim robot shadow. Orange: az 340, el 83, darkness 0.75, overlap 0.21 (weak). Same one-darkness + 3 px rule as the exterior view (issues #2–#5).
Resolution
Rendered at 640×360 then area-shrunk to the policy's 320×180, like the real frames.
Hand-set
Mosaic frames (before the first touch) and light-fit frames (18:73:3 default, orange's approach).
Evidence
Recovers 2.6 of the 3.9 cm planning gap (G7); D2 success 4 → 9/33 (p 0.13), lifted 11 → 24 (p 0.001); blur fix 9 → 9. ~1.3 cm still open (checklist 4.2 not passing).
Generalisation risk
High for looks, but it fails softly: the scene still builds and runs, and the error shows as the policy aiming off. Every piece was fitted on one episode: a new wrist camera needs its own library, short or static approaches leave mosaic holes, and the light fit may find no shadow to fit. Its effect can only be judged by a GPU image swap per scene (4.2).

21 · Camera search on a sphere (plan 10-28)

Trigger: user, 10-28: "can we identify how far the camera should be that would give us a sphere kind of thing, then we keep the camera moving in the small sector of that sphere and wherever the overlap is maximum should be the best camera angle", then "can you write up the plan then start build and run the same over multiple scenes". Context: every fit so far gets the scene right near the objects but leaves the exterior table tilted (pitch 3.0° orange, 8.0° c11; roll 0.5° / 3.0°; §20e), which probably hides a position error of a few cm (fixing c11's pitch while keeping the objects in place moves the camera ~7 cm). The local optimiser can also drift (c9: 54°).

21a · Method

  • Parametrisation. Look-at point P = the mean of the real grasp points (fingertip at each first grasp, FK). The camera sits at distance r from P, at azimuth / elevation (az, el) around it, with roll about its viewing axis; its orientation is chosen so P lands on the pixel where its real object is (the grasp points' target masks in frame 0). Four searched numbers: az, el, r, roll; the grasp constraint fixes the other two.
  • Score (lower is better; each part also reported on its own): (1) robot outline distance over 12 (coarse) to 30 (fine) frames spread over the episode and over distances from the camera; (2) grasp points to their objects in frame 0 (px); (3) the exterior table, rebuilt with MoGe scaled by the grasp points, must be level (tilt in degrees) and, where a trusted wrist-stereo height exists, at that height (cm). The outline alone is nearly flat along the pitch ↔ position trade-off; (3) is what breaks it.
  • Search. Coarse grid around the start (az, el ± 10°, r ± 15 cm, roll at the start), then a fine grid around the best (roll ± 5°). Wider sector for scenes whose start is poor. Start = the better of DROID's recorded pose and the outline-only fit.
  • Outputs. Best camera (calib_search.json, calib.json format); position and angle change from the start; heatmaps of the score and of each part over az × el (a sharp peak = pinned; a ridge = the data can't decide that direction → clicks or camera 2).
  • Verification (not used by the score): rebuild with --depth grasp; real releases inside the containers; scene_checks; outline overlap on frames not used in the search.
