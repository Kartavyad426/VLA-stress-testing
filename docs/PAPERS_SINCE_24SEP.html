0 · Headline

In five lines
  • Interpretability via SAE features (Dr. VLA) was not adopted. The features would each need their own causal test before they could be trusted as labels; the paper's own evidence for "general vs memorized" is partly circular.
  • Runtime monitoring (Sentinel/STAC, FAIL-Detect, VLA-Corrector) supplied our root-signal ideas. STAC is pre-registered (R-059) and waits for GPU time; only cheap single-sample proxies have run.
  • Data-targeting papers (Mulligan, Jung & Park, FLARE) moved our novelty claim. "Failure-targeted beats uniform at a matched budget" is now published (Mulligan), so ours is mechanism-targeted vs failure-targeted.
  • One adaptation paper became an experiment: the 4K-parameter visual recalibration (FTM, 2512.02902) is R-060, pre-registered, blocked on a render check.
  • Data: no public dataset labels where a failure is decided. The best external source is podolinsky's GR00T-N1.7 LIBERO rollouts (10 sampled plans per step, VLM onset labels); work on it starts today (session impl2).

1 · STAC: what we actually know

Careful wording

Safe line: "early signs the policy is confidently wrong on start-pose failures; the proper multi-sample STAC test is pre-registered and waiting for GPU time."

  • What STAC is (Sentinel paper). Compare the distribution of the policy's planned actions over the steps two consecutive chunks share, using several noise samples per step. GR00T predicts 40 steps and executes 16, so 24 steps overlap; nothing has to be added to the stack.
  • 09-28, single-sample STAC on stored R-041 chunks: swamped by the policy's own sampling noise (noise floor ~7 vs a perturbed-vs-nominal gap ~1); AUROC at chance. This is why R-059 uses ≥ 32 seeded samples.
  • 10-09, E1 (R-059 part A, single sample, CPU): on start-pose perturbations, failures were more self-consistent than successes (AUROC 0.20 at the first forward, CI 0.11–0.30). That fits "confidently wrong". It may also be a phase artefact: rollouts are compared at the same forward index, not the same stage of the task. The check that settles it (E1-phase) has not been run. Camera yaw: no signal (0.40–0.60).
  • "The policy lacks data in the regions where it fails" is our hypothesis, not a STAC result. It is supported by the fine-tuning work: the adapters fitted their own training starts (first chunk 27–30% of the way to nominal) but moved new starts only ~10%, so coverage, not volume, looks like the limit (D20, R-056 post-mortem).
  • Not yet run: R-059 B (seeded multi-sample smoke) and C1 (240 start-pose rollouts, only if STAC ≥ 3× the noise floor). All results above are exploratory and not val-checked (round exempted by the user, 10-09).

Dr. VLA / SAE features
Swann et al., Stanford, arXiv:2603.19183 (repo drvla)		rejected Sparse-autoencoder features in π0.5 / OpenVLA are interpretable (79% of 120 sampled) and steerable; removing 4 "general" features drops real-robot success 39/40 → 0/40. Labels and the general/memorized classifier use the same metrics, so they partly check themselves. The ablation is confounded by how often and how strongly a feature fires (no matched-signal control). Steering tests are small (n = 5) and tuned per task. Each feature would need its own causal test to be used as a label: it narrows the search to candidates, it is not a control panel. Also not adopted: comparing SAE features before and after our fine-tune to explain the scene-1327 regression. HANDOFF_6Oct §SAE, Decision_28Sep
Simulator alternatives
Isaac Lab-Arena v0.3, Cosmos Transfer 2.5, MuJoCo Warp, RoboVerse		rejected Richer sims and generative re-rendering. Either don't fit 8 GB (Isaac Sim 6.0 needs 16 GB VRAM; Cosmos Transfer 65 GB) or change the pixels/physics GR00T was trained on. ROBOGATE measured a GR00T LIBERO-Spatial checkpoint at 97.65% in MuJoCo and 0% on Isaac Sim scenes. We stay on LIBERO-Plus on MuJoCo. SIM_ALTERNATIVES_PIPELINE
IDM + world models as a way to raise success
GR00T N1 neural trajectories, DreamGen, Seer, UWM, VLA-RFT, AnyPos		rejected as a fix · kept as a tool Inverse-dynamics models label generated or human video with actions. Gains need huge video-generation compute (~105k L40 GPU-h for GR00T N1) or a low-data regime. In sim, actions are already free and exact. Kept: a small IDM as a measuring instrument (commanded vs achieved motion without robot state, gripper events on video-only data); gate tests T0/T1/E3b proposed. IDM_VLA_HORIZONS

Sentinel / STAC
Agia et al., CoRL 2024		pre-registered Temporal consistency between overlapping chunks, thresholds calibrated on successes only (conformal). R-059: STAC at matched forward index vs a clock baseline. GR00T's 24-step overlap means no shadow inference. Our "spread across samples at one moment" probe (S4) equals the paper's worst baseline (TPR 0.33), so it is demoted to a weak covariate. Nobody has run STAC on a VLA; it is an extension, and may not transfer. Status in §1. SENTINEL_METHODOLOGY §13
FAIL-Detect
Xu et al., TRI, RSS 2025, arXiv:2503.08558 (full read + code)		borrowed Works on a frozen checkpoint: train only a small scorer on the policy's observation embedding, calibrate a time-varying band on successful rollouts. Lesson for GR00T: use the pre-adapter image features (S1), not what the action head conditions on (S2 does not move under vision perturbations, R-037). Expect it to detect perturbations, not predict failure (R-037). Not run; no R-number. Code is CC BY-NC, so reimplement. FAIL_DETECT_MATHS
VLA-Corrector
Pan et al., ZJU / Alibaba, arXiv:2607.01804 (full read + code)		borrowed The "predicted vs actual change" idea became root signal E3 (commanded vs achieved motion), at action level. Most of its gain is replanning sooner (48.7 → 60.35); the gradient-guidance part adds ~4 points and is negative on the Hard split. Its monitor can't fire in the first ~15 control steps, which is where our start-pose failures are decided. A 6-arm runtime-correction test was sketched, not scheduled. VLA_CORRECTOR_MATHS
"VLAs are more generalizable than you think" (FTM / FLA)
Li et al., CVPR 2026, arXiv:2512.02902 (full read)		pre-registered A 4,096-parameter per-channel affine on visual tokens recovers most of a camera-viewpoint gap. Became R-060 (camera and noise rows; FTM vs an action-head fix at matched data). Its "LoRA" baseline is mostly a full SigLIP fine-tune (414.8M of 467M), and its Table 3 FTM average is misprinted (90.5 vs 93.2). It is a camera fix, not a start-pose fix. Mulligan
Ankile et al., Stanford, arXiv:2610.05882 (full read)		borrowed Published version of our failure-targeted arm: retry the initial states that failed, fill the rest by coverage. Its Alg. 2 is how we would build that arm (D23). About half its simulated gain is better coverage, not failure targeting. Moves our novelty claim to mechanism-targeted (which frames to label, from an internal mechanism) vs failure-targeted. MULLIGAN_TARGETED_INIT
Jung & Park, initial-pose dependence
KIST, arXiv:2608.11769 (full read)		borrowed Same question as ours (GR00T start-pose failures, wrist camera matters). We borrow its equal-budget coverage control and a physical joint-substitution check. Weaker instruments (behavioural metrics, out-of-distribution masking); its targeted fix worked at one pose without a matched control. Our splice attribution, time localisation and first-chunk rescue are not in it. JUNG_PARK_INITPOSE
FLARE
Zhao et al., CVPR 2026, arXiv:2608.26645 (full read)		borrowed Its "bridging" (drive from an off-distribution pose back onto a demo) is the same primitive as our bridge-and-join data option. Diagnosis is a VLM classifying videos, not mechanistic. VLM-mined resets add 140 → 147 / 200 (p = 0.51). Never tests start-pose perturbation. FLARE_RECOVERY
Demo-generation literature
LIBERO-Plus training set, MimicGen, DART, DAgger, CCIL, VLAMotor, RECALL, DATAFARM		borrowed Replaying demo actions from moved starts barely helps (LIBERO-Plus's own set: +8.6 pp on robot initial state), so we dropped it as the main source. Next source: bridge-and-join (a one-skill MimicGen-lite) or a scripted expert with DART-style noise. "Find failures, then synthesise data" is not a new idea in itself (2026 papers do it). Wichmann & Hill (psychometric fitting), Geirhos et al., covering arrays, adaptive design		adopted Treat the failure boundary as a probability: fit success vs magnitude as a curve with a lapse term and scene effects; spend replicates where the 50% crossing is least certain; drop bisection. Covering arrays kept for later, for discrete factors only. SWEEP_DESIGN_LITERATURE
Flow-matching background

5 · Surveys and landscapes

SurveyDateQuestionConclusionStatus
RL gyms for VLAs09-24What exists for closed-loop "find errors, mine data, retrain" improvement? "Gym" means three things: env suites (crowded), RL substrates (emerging: RLinf-VLA, SimpleVLA-RL), environments-as-a-service (none for robots; 0 of 38 vendors). Nobody ships the full loop; the closest attempt (AgenticRobotics, 2608.07555) found the final checkpoint beat every measurement-driven selector. Everyone breaks at diagnosis and deciding what data to collect, which is where our harness sits. positioning
Human video to policy09-25What is egocentric, captioned assembly-line video (no robot actions) worth? Actions transfer, pixels don't (R-039: every perturbation reaches the action via image tokens). Recommended route B′: retarget hand pose to actions, use captions to segment, render observations from sim or robot. Needs a metric-scale source (stereo, depth or one measured fixture). open (scale source, policy choice)
Suites and checkpoints09-28Can we evaluate beyond one LIBERO suite? GR00T work is all on libero_spatial (2,712 rollouts). Every published LIBERO number is a fine-tune. MINERVA covers all 40 tasks and reproduced; π0.5 needs a bigger GPU. The perturbation knobs carry over; localisation needs goal-based scoring and stage awareness. informs roster
Robustness of experiments09-28Where is GR00T not robust, against the benchmark's own ranges? Start pose: not robust, measured (0.37 at 0.5 rad). Layout and language: fragile, no magnitude axis. Camera yaw: degrades (0.93 → 0.58–0.73 at 50–75°). Distance, pitch, −yaw, lighting, noise: unknown (sweeps stopped short; lighting knob only brightens). internal map

Failure-mining relevance09-29What unit do people mine failures over, and what matters to a client? Measure at (policy, task, perturbation, size); report weighted by how often each condition occurs in the client's deployment × failure cost (the self-driving ODD/exposure idea). Benchmarks (LIBERO-Plus/PRO, COLOSSEUM, LIBERO-CTRL) and failure searchers (RoboGate, FATE-VLA, RoboMD) don't weight by deployment. Mostly abstract-level reading. framing
