E. Counting rule

A rollout failure counts as a policy failure only if: the expert solves that variant (#2) and a do-nothing policy doesn't (#25); the scene passes A (#3–#6, #24) and the wrist-view swap (#11); there is no sustained blocked command (#17); and #19–#20 show the policy behaves on real frames and our images don't move its plan. Anything else is unattributed. Today no orange failure is fully attributable, because #11 does not pass yet; the early release is attributed to the policy through #19 (it shows on real frames).

F. Blind spots of the 10-15 version (now rules)

  1. The wrist view was judged by eye (#11 passed on coverage); the image swap showed it moves the plan by ~4 cm. Rule: visuals pass only through an image swap.
  2. No check for trivial variants: a do-nothing policy "solved" one. Rule: #25.
  3. Camera pose was checked only by robot-outline overlap, which is blind along the camera's rays (a 6 cm table offset looked perfect). Rule: geometry is validated from a second viewpoint and against real robot coordinates (#3, #6, #24).
  4. The bowl's 2.18 cm settle failure was read as "borderline"; it was a placement bug. Rule: a failed check is a bug until shown otherwise.
