1 · Goal

  • A feature for external customers that can be sold. An intermediate step is fine, and even preferred, if it leads to a monetisable asset later. Internal research is easier to justify when that asset is promised down the line.
  • The commercial framing is prim's inference, and it matches what the user described: teams training VLAs who have rollouts and demos but no failure labels. We find where and why the policy fails and deliver labelled failure instances plus targeted data.

3 · The miner (feeds the retraining offering)

  • Input: mining-spec v0 instances (mining_spec_v0_schema.png). The search keys are root (phase, failure mode, step), situation.fields (with tolerance and held_fixed) and success.predicate.
  • Search space: our corpus of human plus simulated demos, or the customer's.
  • Output: corrective demos for scenarios that previously failed.
  • Search method: deferred by the user.
  • Order (agreed): run a basic spec-driven retrieval first. Use simulated generation (ROOT_TO_GOAL_CORRECTIVE_DEMOS) only where retrieval finds nothing within tolerance. Mining stays in, even though generation might make it unnecessary.

4 · What already exists (from prim, 10-12)

  • MINING_SPEC_DESIGN D14, "Success query = situation + success".
  • MINING_SPEC_V1_DESIGN §1, "Dataset search": ms_v1_search.py, configs/mining_spec_v1/search.json, N7 tolerances (±1.5 cm toy hit) with a mandatory tolerance sweep. Image-embedding search is out of v1. The doc awaits the user's approval and nothing is coded.
  • ROOT_TO_GOAL §2, S3 "Nearest demo": same task, then same phase (split at gripper events). It matches a 5–10-step window ending at the root, on relative end-effector pose in the target-object frame plus gripper width, normalised by the spread across demos. It keeps the top 3–5; anything beyond the threshold is a coverage gap. Threshold and k are open (§5).
  • So the miner is essentially D14 search plus S3, run on a client or large corpus. The new parts are the product layer below.

5 · PoC shape and open points

  • Coverage and improvement go together (user): "with coverage you can't improve and without improvement coverage don't matter". Coverage means the retrieved demos succeed when replayed from the failure situation. Improvement means retraining on them beats a matched broad-coverage set.
  • Proposed failure target, not confirmed: mid-episode roots (grasp miss or slip, wrong object, stall), because v1's rule-based roots produce these and nominal human demos can plausibly cover them. Start pose is weak: nominal demos don't reach large offsets, and re-homing is a free fix. Camera shift would need augmentation as well as retrieval.
  • Corpus scale: 500 LIBERO-Spatial demos now. LIBERO-90/100 share objects and scenes and could be the large-corpus test.
  • Gaps: no retrieval literature survey exists. STRAP, CUPID, DataMIL and Behavior Retrieval are mentioned across five docs; FlowRetrieval and SAILOR appear nowhere. LIBERO demo replay in our environment is unverified (T0), and that blocks the coverage check.
  • Lesson to carry over: retraining pilot R-053..R-058 was negative because the data covered the wrong places, not because there was too little of it.

7 · v0 brainstorm: non-retraining features

Added 2026-10-12 · session brain · sources: PAPERS_SINCE_24SEP, AMBITIOUS_DIRECTIONS (+ review), PAPERS_WORKING_NOTES. We discuss these one at a time and mark each one build or park. The discussion for each offering is logged in BRAINSTORM.html, one section per offering.

First read (brain, not decided)

  • Option 1 looks strongest as the core. The 09-24 RL-gym survey found 0 of 38 vendors offering robot environments-as-a-service, and found that everyone breaks at diagnosis, which is where our harness sits. It works with API access only and uses what is already verified.
  • The report can lead into the rest. The v0 schema's fixability field already includes operational and augmentation. So the envelope report can end in operational fixes ("keep the camera within ±20°", "re-home before grasp"), with options 2–4 sold as add-ons.
  • Credibility PoC (from AMBITIOUS_DIRECTIONS, cross-policy section): run only the black-box tools on GR00T as if it were a black box, and score them against our white-box ground truth (wrist camera for start pose, first chunk decides). MINERVA is the second architecture.
