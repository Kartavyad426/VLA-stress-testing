Where we went beyond the doc

Most of the extra work is mechanism: which camera or input channel carries each perturbation, whether it survives into the action (R-037/R-039/R-042), and when the episode is decided (R-050/R-057). That is stronger evidence for a manifest's "trigger" column than the doc's clustering approach. It hasn't yet been turned into the manifest.

If you want to close the doc before expanding

  1. Let the R-055 rerun and then R-056 finish. That gives the before/after the core thesis requires.
  2. Write the manifest from what already exists: the R-047 boundaries, the pathway results and the R-057 findings. A few rows, each with its evidence, is enough.
  3. Fill in the human-review sheets (runs/lplus_fail_groot/adjudication.csv, runs/lplus_fail_minerva/adjudication.csv). Even two people each labelling 40 failures gives the agreement score.
  4. Re-freeze a regression set from the R-047/R-052 failures.
  5. Optional: an object-layout dose-response curve, so all three priority axes have one.

Once those are in, you'd be free to expand: a data-budget curve, R-043 long-horizon tasks, or R-040's comparison of ways to choose training data.
