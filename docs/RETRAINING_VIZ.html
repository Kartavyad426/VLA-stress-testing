2 · Loss curves

The x-axis is the optimizer step in every chart. The lerobot runs log a loss: value every 10 micro-batches, which is the mean of those 10 micro-batch losses (lerobot AverageMeter, reset at each log; third_party/lerobot/src/lerobot/scripts/lerobot_train.py:698,792). With accumulation 8, that is one point every 1.25 optimizer steps. The micro-step is read from the tqdm counter (N/16000) and divided by 8. The r = 64 cached-feature run logs the mean of the last 80 micro-batch losses every 10 optimizer steps (experiments/r056_train.py:478). The y-axis is logarithmic. Faint dots are the raw logged values, and the solid line is the median of each 50-optimizer-step block. LR and gradient norm are plotted in separate panels below each loss chart, never on a second y-axis.

All full-length runs: block-median loss overlaid

Sources: runs/overnight/R056fix_train.log, R058_r4_train.log, R058_r64_cached_train.log, R056_train.log (cyclic). Raw loss values, with medians computed on this page.

3 · Validation per checkpoint

Ten validation rollouts per checkpoint, the same 10 starts for every arm. The rule that picks the checkpoint is "max val success, then min median closest approach, then later step" (selection.json rule). A ring marks the selected checkpoint. For the cyclic run, only the opt500 checkpoint was evaluated before the eval was killed.
