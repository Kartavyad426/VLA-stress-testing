Robustness of experiments

GR00T N1.7 · nvidia/gr00t17-lerobot-libero_spatial-640 · LIBERO-Plus fork of libero_spatial · written 2026-09-28 from RESULTS.md (R-041, R-047, R-052) and docs/PERTURBATION_MAP.html

Section 0 counts what has been run so far, task by task. Section 1 lists every perturbation the benchmark applies and its range. The rest of the page asks two questions. First, given how far each perturbation was pushed compared with the benchmark's range, where is the policy not robust? Second, which rows cannot be judged yet because the sweep stopped short or used a different knob? The second half covers the status of the continuous sweep programme: start from a known success and push one axis until it fails.

Two findings from decoding the source.

Our lighting sweep never reached the benchmark's range. The harness knob sets diffuse × (1 + m), clipped to 1 (vla_harness/envs/libero_env.py:444–448). With nominal diffuse 0.8, it saturates at m = 0.25, so R-041's "0–3×" is really 1.00–1.25× brightening, flat beyond 0.25. That matches R-041 finding action boundaries below 0.19 on six scenes and none elsewhere. The benchmark mostly dims (median 0.62×) and changes direction and shadows. The knob covers about 4% of the benchmark's lighting variants.

Our noise instances used three of the five families. The ten mapped noise instances are indices 16, 17, 18, 20, 29 and 39: Gaussian blur at severities 6–10, zoom blur at 9, and fog at 9. Motion blur and glass blur (161 of 351 variants; glass is the largest family) were never tested. docs/PERTURBATION_MAP.html §2 says "severities to 50" and "severities 16 to 39"; those are indices, not severities, and the sentence needs correcting in experiments/compile_map.py:409.

Layout and language: fragile, but only as instances

Both rows fail most of their instances (layout 8/10, language 7/10). There is no magnitude knob, so the question "how far can it be pushed" is not defined yet for them. The layout failure is carried by the agent view (0.55) and is not rescued by a corrected first chunk. For language, a rewrite moves the first action by a median of 1.42, and by 2.81 on R-038's null-prompt scenes.

4 · Measured, but possibly too kind: camera yaw

Pooled success is 0.93 up to 20°, about 0.80 at 30–40°, and 0.58–0.73 from 50° to 75°. Only 1 of 8 scored scenes (between-plate-ramekin) falls to ≤ 0.2; the prediction was 8 of 10. The harness knob orbits the vertical through the camera's look-at point, so the scene stays centred in the image. LIBERO-Plus orbits the world origin, which also translates the scene across the frame. The benchmark's cone is therefore probably harsher than this curve. R-052 measures it.

5 · Unknown: the range stopped short, or the knob measured something else

  1. Camera distance. We reached ~1.4×; the benchmark goes to 2.0×. Both failing campaign distance instances lie beyond our maximum. R-041 still succeeds at max on 9/10 scenes, but that is at a distance the benchmark barely uses.
  2. Camera pitch. Never swept, yet it is present in 4 of 10 mapped camera failures.
  3. Negative yaw. Never swept. Two campaign failures sit there. R-052 also runs positive yaw only.
  4. Look-away rotation. No knob exists.
  5. Lighting. "Robust to lighting" (10/10 at "3×") is an artefact of the knob. It only brightens, and it clips at 1.25× nominal from m = 0.25 onwards. The benchmark mostly dims (median 0.62×, some variants with both lights off) and moves the light direction and shadows. Of all the gaps, this one is the most likely to be misread as good news.
  6. Noise. Our instances are Gaussian blur 6–10, zoom blur 9 and fog 9 (indices 16–39). Motion blur and glass blur, 161 of the 351 variants, were never tested, and there is no severity sweep in any family. Rescue is unreadable at 1 failure in 10.
  7. Texture. The two campaign failures barely reproduce (0 and 1 of 10 across seeds). This is not evidence of robustness, only an unreadable row at this n.
Baseline caveat. On-ramekin fails with nothing perturbed under one noise seed, on both R-047 axes. On-wooden-cabinet fails once at yaw 0. R-029's 100/100 used one noise seed per episode, so "start from a success" is not 100% across seeds on every scene. Those scenes are excluded from the pooled numbers above.

6 · Status of the continuous sweep programme

The design: start from a known success (the R-029 control, 100/100), move one axis at a time, and find where the action first departs from nominal and where the outcome flips.

  • R-041: bisection, one rollout per point. DONE 2026-09-23. 4 axes × 10 scenes, 240 rollouts, magnitude-0 40/40. The action departs at the smallest resolvable magnitude on every axis except light. Outcome boundaries were found only on some scenes: yaw 21–40° (top-drawer, table-center), distance 0.31–0.4 m (on-ramekin), start pose 0.15–0.5 rad (stove, table-center, top-drawer), light none. Still succeeding at max: yaw 8/10, distance 9/10, light 10/10, start pose 7/10. Limit: one rollout per point, while outcomes flip 30–40% under jitter, so a bracket is a sample, not a threshold. docs/R041_RESULTS.html.
  • R-047: replicated probability curves. DONE 2026-09-28 14:46. This corrected R-041's ranges to the benchmark's on start pose (0–0.5 rad) and yaw (0–75°), 720 rollouts. Results are in sections 3 and 4; 2 of 6 expectations held. Yaw kept the old pivot by decision D5, for comparability with R-041.
  • Single-draw STAC on R-041's stored chunks. DONE (CPU, vla-a5). The noise floor (~7) is far above ‖P−N‖ (~1), and AUROC is at chance. This motivates the distributional readout (D2), which is designed but has no R-number yet.
  • R-047 part 2 (distance to 2.0×, pitch). DEFERRED, superseded by R-052.
  • R-052: the benchmark's own camera cone. PRE-REGISTERED 2026-09-28, NOT QUEUED. Axes camera_bench_yaw_deg 0–75 (the same grid as R-047, for a point-for-point comparison), camera_bench_scale 1.0–2.0, and camera_bench_pitch_deg 0–15. 10 scenes × 4 seeds, ~760 rollouts, ~9.5 h GPU. The knobs call each scene's own LIBERO-Plus _setup_camera and match 7 real views to 1e-4 (tests/test_bench_camera.py). Blocker: experiments/queue_r052.sh has not been written. The GPU is idle now, since R-047 released the lock at 14:46.
  • Not registered anywhere:
    • negative yaw
    • look-away rotation (a knob is needed)
    • a benchmark-matched lighting knob (dimming, direction, shadows)
    • a severity sweep, 1–10, for each of the five noise families
    • layout sweeps over distractor count (1–5) and displacement (1–5 cm), both already ordinal in the benchmark
    • a magnitude axis for language

What the programme has established so far

  • The first action departs from nominal at almost any magnitude on every axis. The outcome is much more tolerant and scene-dependent. So the fixed boundary we set out to find exists only in the outcome, and only on some scenes.
  • Start pose is where the programme answered its question over the benchmark's range. Every camera axis still needs to be re-measured on the benchmark's geometry (R-052).

7 · What the literature says about this design

Summary of the sweep session's review, docs/SWEEP_DESIGN_LITERATURE.html (2026-09-28). Citations and details are there. Two of its 2026 papers were read from abstracts only.

  • Is it a continuous space with definite boundaries? Not in the outcome. The review's answer is to model each axis as a psychometric curve: a logistic success probability against magnitude, with a lapse term (on-ramekin fails at zero perturbation, so p(0) < 1) and scene effects partially pooled (hierarchical logistic). Report the 50% point with a CI, the transition width and the lapse. ‖P−N‖ is a local-sensitivity covariate, never a boundary. This matches what R-041 and R-047 found.
  • Keep one-axis replicated grids; drop bisection. Add an adaptive second stage that samples where the fitted curve is most uncertain, near its 50% point (level-set estimation, the "straddle" rule). Robotics evidence puts the saving at 20–40% of rollouts, not 10×.
  • Interactions matter. Add a small layer of pairwise cells, because combined perturbations cost far more than their parts (Colosseum; LIBERO-Plus's own compositional result).
  • Minimal-set coverage (covering arrays, t-way testing) fits the discrete factors (texture, lighting draws, language rewrites) later, with ≥ 4 seeds per row. It assumes deterministic faults, so it does not fit Bernoulli outcomes over continuous axes on its own.
  • Statistical claims: Clopper–Pearson intervals, or sequential tests for comparing policies, over point success rates.

Its proposed R-052 (revised 2026-09-28 after the benchmark facts in §1): ≈920 rollouts, ≈11.8 h, against the 760 registered.

  • an 8-seed nominal anchor;
  • yaw at both signs ±20/40/60/75;
  • scale 1.15–2.0;
  • pitch 15° alone as a 4-seed diagnostic only (40 rollouts), since the benchmark always pairs pitch with yaw;
  • about 100 adaptive rollouts;
  • a stage of about 160 joint cells, led by yaw × pitch 15° at both yaw signs and then yaw × scale at negative yaw;
  • a calibration check against about 60 real LIBERO-Plus camera variants.

A lean version (≈780) keeps only the yaw × pitch joint cells.

This follows section 1: the benchmark uses pitch only at 15°, always with yaw, and its hard camera variants are mostly negative-yaw and pitched.

8 · Recommended order

  1. Amend R-052, then write queue_r052.sh and run it. The amendment is a timestamped pre-registration change taking in the §7 proposal: both yaw signs, pitch 15° always paired with yaw, scale from 1.15×, and an adaptive stage. It clears the distance, pitch and pivot gaps in one pass (~10–12 h).
  2. Replace the lighting knob. Add a dimming axis (total diffuse 1.0 → 0) and a direction/shadow axis modelled on LIBERO-Plus's light XMLs, then pre-register a lighting sweep. It is the gap most likely to be misread as robust, and the current knob is clipped.
  3. Noise: a severity sweep of 1–10 in each of the five families, agent view only (R-051 shows the wrist is inert here), starting with motion and glass blur, which were never tested. It is cheap (a post-render filter, no new knob) and gives the rescue arm failures to work with.
  4. Layout sweeps over distractor count and displacement. The benchmark's variants already define both axes, and layout is the most fragile instance row.
  5. Only after that: look-away knobs, and a magnitude for language (word count or edit distance).
  6. Fix the map text: experiments/compile_map.py:409 calls noise indices "severities"; send to impl.
